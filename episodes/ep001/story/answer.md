**Question:** A lender offers Nora a US mortgage refinance (new lower-rate loan paying off the old) for $5,124 in fees. What rate cut repays that within three years, and why do smaller mortgages need bigger cuts?

**Answer:** About 1/3 point for Anjali ($655k), 1/2 point for Nora ($375k), over 1 point for Walt ($115k).

**Takeaways:**
1. Fees barely shrink with loan size; savings do.
2. Division ignores the restarted 30-year clock.
3. How long you stay decides it.
