**Câu hỏi của tập:** *For a private grad loan, how often did a variable rate that starts lower end up costing more than a fixed rate — and how bad did it get when it did?*

## Logline A — "người như tôi" + lời hứa đáp án
> Since July 1, 2026, new graduate students can no longer get federal Grad PLUS loans, and the federal loan they can still get stops at a yearly limit. Students who need more turn to private lenders, who usually offer two kinds of rate: a variable rate that can start lower, or a fixed rate that never changes. This video takes one illustrative $50,000, 10-year private loan — 7.5% variable against 9% fixed — and replays it through every 10-year stretch of US interest rates since 1954, to show how often the lower start ended up costing more in total, how bad the worst stretch was, and how the answer changes when the head start is bigger, smaller, or zero. History, not a forecast.

Claim: `ctx_plus_end`, `ctx_unsub_annual` (không nói số trong logline), `loan`, `term`, `var_start`, `fixed_rate`, `first_start`, `share_all`/`share_early`/`share_late` (đáp án), `worst_diff`, `gap*` (phạm vi b).
Tiêu đề nháp **A1** (58 ký tự): *Need Private Grad Loans? Variable vs Fixed Through History* — kiểu móc: "người như tôi" + lời hứa đáp án.

## Logline B — nghịch lý (sự thật lạ có claim)
> Take a variable-rate grad loan that starts at 7.5% and replay it through every 10-year stretch of US interest rates since 1954. In about three of every four stretches, its rate climbed above a 9% fixed rate at some point. Yet over the whole loan it cost more far less often — in more than one in four stretches that began before 1981, and about one in thirty after. This video follows one illustrative $50,000, 10-year loan through both kinds of history, including the worst stretch, which began in April 1977, to show how both facts can be true. History, not a forecast.

Claim: `share_rate_above_fixed` (76.2% "about three of every four"), `share_early` (28.4% "more than one in four"), `share_late` (3.5% "about one in thirty"), `worst_start` (April 1977), `var_start`, `fixed_rate`, `loan`, `term`, `first_start`.
Tiêu đề nháp **B1** (60 ký tự): *Why a Variable Loan Can Rise Above Fixed and Still Cost Less* — kiểu móc: nghịch lý.
