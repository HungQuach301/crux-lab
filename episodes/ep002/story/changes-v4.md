## Root cause and fix

In v3, every sentence that mentioned Leah's pair again treated the both-periods rule as "restate the full set". As a result, 28.4% / 3.5% / $11,219 was spoken in full three times (v3 S05.4, S08.7, S10.4), and $11,219 appeared 5 times. The rule now applies only to a sentence that **states** a share or result. A sentence that **recalls** Leah's pair names it in words ("the split already heard", "her April 1977 worst"). Each share has one fixed spoken form for the whole episode: "76.2%, about 3 in 4", "14.2%, about 1 in 7", "28.4%, more than 1 in 4", "3.5%, about 1 in 30". Shares without a wording use the display only (20.4%, 10.5%), and 0% is "none".
