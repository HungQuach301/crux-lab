**Description — method and limits (C2 v2; text for the upload description, moved out of the S32 narration):**
- Data: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, weekly US averages of the 30-year and 15-year fixed rates (FRED MORTGAGE30US, MORTGAGE15US), 1,833 paired weeks from August 1991 to the week of October 8, 2026. The survey describes borrowers with strong credit and a large down payment; real offers differ in points, fees and terms.
- Method: in every week, the same $320,000 priced as a 15-year loan and as a 30-year loan paid off in 15 years, at that week's own rates; the stack = the difference between the two interest totals.
- Payments are principal and interest only: no property taxes, homeowners insurance, private mortgage insurance (PMI) or tax deductions.
- The bar = the loan's own rate = interest no longer charged on each extra dollar of principal, before taxes. It is not an investment return, and this video does not test whether any investment or market return clears it.
- What a lower required payment is worth in a job loss or an emergency is not measured.
- Tessa and Leo are illustrative. Tessa's numbers hold only at the survey rates of the week of October 8, 2026 on $320,000; another offer gives another stack.
- US only. History, not a forecast. Not advice on which loan to take, or on whether to invest.
