How long did private mortgage insurance last for a buyer with 10% down? We replayed every US purchase month from January 1991 to July 2016 against the payment schedule the law uses. US only. History, not a forecast. On paper is not the same as the insurance being removed. This video does not say whether to buy or wait.

Method: Freddie Mac 30-year weekly average rate (FRED MORTGAGE30US), monthly mean; the latest full month is September 2026. FHFA national purchase-only house price index, not seasonally adjusted (FRED HPIPONM226N). Illustrative loan: 10% down, 360-month fixed, at each purchase month's rate. "On paper" = the balance is at or under 80% of the purchase price times the index change. 307 purchase months, 1991–2016; two-year check: 403 purchase months to July 2024. Law: 12 U.S.C. 4902 (request at 80% of the original value, automatic end at 78%). Removal on current value follows the loan owner's rules (Fannie Mae Servicing Guide B-8.1-04).
Not modeled: what the insurance costs, appraisal fees, rent, local prices, how fast savings grow, each lender's rules.
