It's tempting to set that next to the bond's doubling rate and call it settled. But these rates aren't measured the same way, and bill rates are quoted on a scale where the doubling line sits a little lower. The bigger problem is time: that rate covers one bill, and the roll lives on 80 of them. What decides the race is the bill rate across all 20 years, and that is exactly what nobody knows in advance, us included.
