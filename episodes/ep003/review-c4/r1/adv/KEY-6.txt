There's something else double never promised: what the money will buy. Over 20 years prices rise, sometimes slowly and sometimes fast. To check it, we compared each doubled amount with how far consumer prices rose over the same 20 years. The doubled amount kept its buying power in 58.7 percent of starts. The worst start, January 1966, ended with a double that bought only 58.0 percent of what the original money had bought. For Dana's savings, a start like that would mean twice the dollars buying only a little more than half of what the original sum did.
