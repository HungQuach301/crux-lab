## B — đề tài #2 (tax-4), viết mới, vai: US hourly worker aged 25-50
### B-orig
Same eight extra hours, same forty-eight dollars an hour. Overtime, or a second job: which one leaves more money in your pocket this year? Since 2025, part of overtime pay comes off your federal income tax. Only part. For one worker we modeled, overtime kept about fourteen hundred dollars more a year. By the end, you'll know what a second job has to pay to match it.
### B-degr
Federal law requires time-and-a-half pay for hours worked over forty in a week. Since 2025, a new federal deduction covers part of that overtime pay, up to twelve thousand five hundred dollars a year, through tax year 2028. Payroll tax still applies to all of it. Pay from a second W-2 job is simply added to your wages and taxed at your usual bracket.
