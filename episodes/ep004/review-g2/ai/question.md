# Question

You have two files from a short explainer video (about 8 minutes):
- `narration.txt` — the full spoken text, machine-transcribed, with `[m:ss]` time marks (transcription errors are possible);
- `frames.png` — one still frame every 20 seconds, each labeled with its time.

Do not open anything else. Answer in English, briefly:

(a) In at most 3 sentences: what is this video about, and what does it find?

(b) What would a typical viewer take away as something to *do*? Quote the lines that come closest to advice, if any.

(c) Score each from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong), with one sentence of reason:
  1. Hook (the first 30 seconds)
  2. Story (setting, person, question, answer)
  3. Pictures (do the visuals carry the meaning?)
  4. Pacing (which stretches, if any, would you skip? give times)
  6. Would you watch another video from this channel?

(d) List up to 3 moments (with times) where a viewer is most likely to get confused, and why.
