# C1 — logline (máy, đề tài khuyến nghị #3 `topics-r1/machine/tax-2/`)

**L1:** A married couple bought their home in 2000 and assume a sale would be tax-free, because the $500,000 limit on home-sale profits sounds like more than they could ever make. But that limit has not changed since 1997, and home prices have more than tripled in many cities. We replay 26 years of home prices in 12 metro areas to find, for each one, the 2000 purchase price above which a home that rose like the local average now has a profit over the limit: a number you can check against your own.

Claim: `excl_joint_limit_usd`, `exclusion_effective_month`, `buy_year`, `growth_*` (> 3 ở 9/12 metro: LA, SD, Seattle, Boston, NY, Miami, Denver, Phoenix, Dallas — "more than tripled in many cities"; chỉ số FHFA, không phải căn nhà), `threshold_joint_*`, `sale_quarter`.
