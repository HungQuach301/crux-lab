# retire-1 claim risk: level annuity check vs 2% yearly raise

Analyst's guess before running the data: a 2% raise would keep up in roughly a third of 20-year windows. Actual: 2.4% (only 1947-49 starts).

## Phrasings that overstate the claim
- "A 2% raise never keeps up with inflation." It kept up in 17 of 715 windows; say "almost never since the late 1940s" and give the count.
- "The 2% option is a bad deal" or "pick the level check". Pricing is not modeled: the starting 2% check is lower, and total dollars received depend on the insurer's quote and lifespan. The data speak only to buying power of the rising check.
- "You will lose X% of your buying power." Report history ("in the median 20-year stretch..."), not a forecast for the viewer's retirement.
- "Inflation averages 3.1%." It is the median 20-year rate of 1947-2026 windows, not an expectation.

## The episode must not
- Recommend a payout option, insurer or product, or say a CPI-linked annuity is "better".
- Predict future inflation or say "with inflation at X% today, you will...".
- Treat 715 overlapping windows as 715 independent trials.

## Data limits
- CPI-U all items (CPIAUCNS, not seasonally adjusted); retirees' own baskets (health care, housing) can differ.
- October 2025 CPI is blank in the source (no release); windows touching it are skipped (one 20-year and one 25-year start).
- Pinned FRED CSV through Aug 2026. History, not a forecast.
