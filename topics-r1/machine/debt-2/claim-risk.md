# debt-2 claim risk

**Phrasings that would overstate the claim**
- "Variable rates are safe" or "variable wins 86% of the time". Most of the windows come from the 1981-2021 falling-rate era. In 1954-1980 starts, the variable loan cost more in 28.4% of windows, and the worst case was $11,219 extra on $50,000. Always show both regimes and the worst case together.
- "Your private loan would have…". The model uses a T-bill proxy index, a fixed margin and no cap. Real loans use SOFR-type indexes and caps, and are priced by credit.
- "1.5 points is enough of a head start." The result is for one specific offer pair (7.5% vs 9%). A smaller gap changes the share.

**What the episode must not say**
- No advice ("take the variable", "lock in"). Present the historical distribution only.
- No forecast of rates, the Fed or SOFR. Do not say "rates are likely to fall from here." Say "history, not a forecast."
- Do not describe 2026 federal loan rules beyond what is needed to set up the viewer. The card mentions caps on federal borrowing only as context, and nothing is computed from them.

**Data limits**
- 3-month T-bill (monthly average, discount basis) stands in for the index. Pre-2018 SOFR does not exist.
- Overlapping windows: 753 start months are not 753 independent trials.
- Repayment starts immediately. In-school deferment, fees, autopay discounts and prepayment are ignored. The variable rate is uncapped (it peaks at 20.6% in the worst path).
