# retire-3 claim risk: roll a 1-year CD or lock a 5-year CD when the 1-year pays more

Analyst's guess before running the data: rolling would win in most inverted starts (higher rate first). Actual: locking ended ahead in 64.4% of inverted start months, 14 of 23 episodes.

## Phrasings that overstate the claim
- "Locking always wins when the curve inverts" or "inversions mean rates will fall." 35.6% of inverted starts favored rolling, some by up to 13.9% (1978-80, mid-1960s, 2022).
- "CD rates show..." The data are Treasury constant-maturity yields, a stand-in for CD rates; CD curves can be inverted when Treasuries are not (as in Aug 2026: 1-year 4.03% vs 5-year 4.38%).
- "64% of the time" without noting overlapping months; also give the episode count (23).
- Any statement about where rates are going now.

## The episode must not
- Tell viewers to lock or roll, or name banks/products.
- Forecast rates or call a turn in the rate cycle.

## Data limits
- 1953-2022 starts; the record is dominated by the 1981-2020 decline in rates, which favored locking.
- The 2022-2024 inversion is mostly unscored (5-year outcomes end after Aug 2026).
- Annual compounding at the start-of-period yield; taxes, early-withdrawal penalties and CD-specific rates ignored. History, not a forecast.
