# debt: discarded candidates

I generated 15 candidate decisions, kept 4 (debt-1 to debt-4) and dropped 11. For each candidate I wrote down a guess before running the data, for example "variable loses ~40% of 10-year windows" (result 14.2%), "T-bills ahead by thousands after tax" (result $285) and "72-month rate ~0.5 pt higher" (result 0.1).

| # | Candidate decision (viewer) | Reason dropped |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026 buyer: 5/1 ARM or 30-year fixed? | Ran the data (5/1 ARM cheaper over 7 years in 88% of 2005-2019 start weeks). Novelty search: answered-with-data ("History Shows That Adjustable-Rate Mortgages Pay Off For Homebuyers Most of the Time", redfin.com/news/arm-versus-fixed-rate-mortgage/). |
| 2 | Card carrier: wait for Fed cuts to lower my APR, or act now? | Ran the data (APR down ~1.2 pts vs fed funds down ~1.7 pts since Aug 2024; spread over prime at a record 15.4). Novelty: answered-with-data (CNBC "Credit card APRs fell by just 0.13% after Fed rate cut"; consumerfinance.gov "Credit card interest rate margins at all-time high"). |
| 3 | Card carrier: consolidate with a 24-month personal loan? | Ran the data (gap 10.3 pts, near the series record). Novelty: answered-with-data. Articles quote the same bank-survey figures (22.15% vs 11.86%). |
| 4 | 3% owner needing $50k: HELOC or cash-out refinance? | Novelty: answered-with-data (worked examples with current rates in many guides). The result is mostly arithmetic, not data. |
| 5 | 2020-21 grad with 2.75% federal loans: prepay or T-bills? | Same mechanism as debt-1. Dropped to keep viewer groups distinct, and the 2020-2023 payment pause blurs the window. |
| 6 | 2023-24 buyer at 7%+: extra principal or T-bills? | Result is obvious (bill yields well below 7%), so it would not differ from the viewer's guess. |
| 7 | 2026 buyer: 15-year or 30-year plus invest the difference? | No reproducible long equity-return series in the allowed sources. Against T-bills the answer is trivially the 15-year. |
| 8 | 2023 buyer waiting for more Fed cuts before refinancing | Widely charted since the Sept 2024 cut (likely answered-with-data). It also invites a forecast framing. Not run. |
| 9 | Card carrier: how long minimum payments take | Card statements already show this by required disclosure. Nothing new from data. |
| 10 | 2021 3% owner: move and re-borrow at 7%, or stay? | Lock-in effect is heavily covered with data. Not run. |
| 11 | 2026 buyer: take a 2-1 temporary buydown counting on a refinance within the buydown years | Duplicates the previous round's refinance-window statistic on the same series (m3 debt-1). |
