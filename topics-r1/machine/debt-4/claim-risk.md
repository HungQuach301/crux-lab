# debt-4 claim risk

**Phrasings that would overstate the claim**
- "Points are a waste half the time." What history shows is that in 53% of start weeks a refinance-sized drop *appeared* before break-even. Whether the point was "wasted" depends on whether the borrower refinanced and on closing costs. Part of the point is still recovered before a refinance.
- "Rates fall within 19 months." The 18.9-month median applies only to the weeks where a drop happened. It is not a typical wait for everyone.
- "Today is like 1998." Weeks starting at 6.5-7.5% cluster in a few periods. Do not imply that those periods resemble now.

**What the episode must not say**
- No advice ("skip the points", "buy the points"). Present break-even arithmetic and the historical frequency only.
- No forecast that rates will or will not fall. Say "history, not a forecast" every time a share is shown.

**Data limits**
- Weekly survey averages, not individual quotes. Borrower-specific spreads, closing costs and lender pricing of points vary.
- Overlapping 60-month windows: 2,635 weeks are not independent trials. About 50 years of history contain a handful of rate cycles.
- Selling or moving before break-even is not modeled, and it would make the point lose more often.
