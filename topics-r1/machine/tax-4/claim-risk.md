# tax-4 claim risk: overtime or a second job at the same hourly amount

**Phrasings that would overstate the claim**
- "Overtime is tax-free." Only the half-time premium ($16 of each $48 hour) is deductible, only from federal income tax, and only up to $12,500. Payroll tax applies in full. Never use the slogan without that correction.
- "Overtime pays $1,408 more." That holds only for the modeled worker: single, $32 regular rate, 400 extra hours, standard deduction, 22% bracket, no state tax. A 12% bracket worker gains less, and a married worker's bracket differs. Present it as the worked example and show the rule (premium x bracket rate).
- "A side job must pay $53." This is the break-even for this example only. For this example the side job must pay about $5 an hour more than the overtime rate. The general rule at the 22% bracket with a 1.5x overtime rate is about a tenth more than the overtime rate (premium x 22% / (1 - 7.65% - 22%) per hour). Say "about".
- Do not describe the deduction as permanent. Under current law it ends after tax year 2028.

**What the episode must not say**
- No advice. Do not say "take the overtime", "skip the side gig" or "ask your boss for overtime". Compare the paths only.
- No predictions about extensions of the rule or about wages.
- No claims about employer behavior, scheduling, or whether overtime is available.

**Data and rule limits**
- The wage is the national average hourly earnings of production and nonsupervisory employees for June 2026 (AHETPI via FRED), rounded to $32. Individual wages vary.
- Only overtime required by federal law (over 40 hours a week) counts. State-only daily overtime, double time beyond 1.5x, and union or employer premiums above the federal requirement do not qualify. Tipped income is excluded.
- The side job is assumed to be W-2 work. Self-employment (1099) work would add employer-side payroll tax and could qualify for other deductions, and the model does not cover it.
- The overtime amount must be reported by the employer. Workers whose employers do not report it separately face documentation issues the model ignores.
