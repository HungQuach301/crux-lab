# debt-1 claim risk

**Phrasings that would overstate the claim**
- "Prepaying a 30-year is a waste of money." No: the extra cost buys a real option (a required payment about $640 lower than the 15-year payment). The episode prices the option; it does not judge it.
- "You will pay $19,464 more." Only at the 2026-09-24 survey rates on $320,000; the historical range is $6,097 to $29,502 and an individual's offer differs.
- "The 30-year rate is always 0.6 point higher." The weekly spread ranged 0.2 to 1.0 point since 1991.

**What the episode must not say**
- No advice ("take the 15-year", "always prepay"). Show the dollar cost next to the flexibility.
- No forecast of mortgage rates or of the spread. Say "history, not a forecast."

**Data limits**
- Weekly survey averages for prime borrowers with about 20% down; points and fees differ by term and lender.
- Payments are principal and interest only; no taxes, insurance, discounting or investing of differences.
- The value of the flexibility (job loss, emergencies) is not measured.
