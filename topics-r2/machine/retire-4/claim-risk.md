# retire-4 claim risk: travel early in retirement or spread trips over 10 years

Analyst's guess before running the data: airfares would lag T-bills in about 70% of 10-year windows, hotels would outpace them. Actual: airfares lagged in 56.8% (waiting cost more in 43.2%), lodging outpaced in 65.2%, a mixed trip about even (52.2%).

## Phrasings that overstate the claim
- "Travel prices don't rise": they do; the result is that safe savings roughly kept pace over history, not that prices were flat.
- "Waiting is free": it is close to a wash on prices only; health and other reasons are not measured.
- "Hotels always outrun savings": 65.2% of lodging windows, mostly in a low-rate period since 1997.

## The episode must not
- Tell retirees to travel now or later, or name travel companies.
- Forecast travel prices, inflation or interest rates.

## Data limits
- National price indexes (quality-adjusted, not seasonally adjusted) for airline fares and lodging only; cruises, tours and other trip costs not covered.
- Fixed half-and-half trip mix; T-bills rolled monthly; taxes ignored.
- Lodging data start Dec 1997: about three independent 10-year periods. History, not a forecast.
