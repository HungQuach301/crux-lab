# retire-2 claim risk: give the down payment now or in 10 years

Analyst's guess before running the data: T-bills would lag home prices in about 70% of 10-year windows since 1975. Actual: 44.6% overall, 69.8% for starts since 1990; the latest decade covered only 64.8%.

## Phrasings that overstate the claim
- "Waiting always costs you a third of the gift": that is the latest decade only; before 1990 waiting money often kept up or pulled ahead.
- "Your child's home will cost X more": the index is national; local prices and a given home differ.
- "Give now" or "wait": the episode measures purchasing power, not the family's needs, the parents' own retirement security, or the child's readiness.

## The episode must not
- Advise parents to give, lend or wait, or recommend amounts.
- Forecast home prices or interest rates.

## Data limits
- National all-transactions index (repeat sales), quarterly; T-bills rolled monthly; taxes on interest ignored (favors waiting money); stocks not modeled.
- Mortgage rates, rent paid while waiting and gift-tax reporting are outside the comparison.
- Overlapping windows since 1975 are about five independent 10-year periods. History, not a forecast.
