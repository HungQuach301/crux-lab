# debt: discarded candidates (round 2)

I listed 16 candidate decisions, ran data on 8, kept 4 (debt-1 to debt-4) and dropped 12. Guesses were written before computing (for example "a 30-year paid like a 15-year costs a few thousand more" — result $19,464 at today's rates; "raises fix a 35% payment in 3-4 years" — result median 82 months; "waiting a year lowers the car payment about half the time" — result 38.2%; "PMI lasts about a decade" — schedule 99 months, index median 23 months).

| # | Candidate decision (viewer) | Reason dropped |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Veteran with 20% down: VA loan with funding fee, or conventional? | Ran data (VA rate ~0.22 pt below conforming 740+ since 2017; fee recouped in ~11 years). Novelty: answered-with-data (valoannetwork.com "VA vs Conventional Rates 2026: Full Cost Breakdown"; Optimal Blue VA gap quoted). |
| 2 | Buyer with a 700-719 score: wait to reach 740 before buying? | Ran data (gap ~0.15 pt). Novelty: answered-with-data (themortgagereports.com "Current Mortgage Rates by Credit Score"; Experian average rates by score). |
| 3 | Loan just over the conforming limit: put more down or take a jumbo? | Ran data (jumbo cheaper on average in 2017-19, 2021 and 2022, dearer in 2020 and since 2023). Novelty: answered-with-data (realcostreport.com "Jumbo vs Conforming Loan Rates 2026"). |
| 4 | Renter: buy now with 5% down, or wait a year to save 10%? | Ran data (waiting lowered the payment in ~53-64% of years) — close to the guess, not decision-changing; new-home median price is a poor proxy. |
| 5 | Low-down-payment buyer: FHA or conventional? | Needs current annual and upfront FHA insurance premiums, which are not in the allowed data or official-domain provisions. |
| 6 | Card carrier: move to a small bank or credit-union card with lower APR? | Answered-with-data by the regulator's own report on large vs small issuer APRs. Not run. |
| 7 | Homeowner funding a $30,000 remodel: personal loan or home-equity line? | Needs an assumed line margin; result (secured line cheaper) matches the guess. Not run. |
| 8 | 3% owner with card debt: cash-out refinance to pay cards? | Same core as round-1 discard (HELOC vs cash-out) and close to the excluded refinance decision. |
| 9 | Flexible buyer: shop in winter or wait for spring? | Seasonality widely answered with data (listing-site "best time to buy" studies). Not run. |
| 10 | Car buyer: 0% dealer financing or the cash rebate? | Mostly loan arithmetic, little data content; calculators everywhere. |
| 11 | Card user who sometimes carries a balance: is a rewards card still worth it? | Pure arithmetic; no rewards data in allowed sources. |
| 12 | Student or parent: Parent PLUS vs other borrowing | Rate formulas depend on auction yields not in the allowed series; no data series for private or home-equity alternatives. |
