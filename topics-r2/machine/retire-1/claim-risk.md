# retire-1 claim risk: prepay a funeral vs keep the money in safe savings

Analyst's guess before running the data: funeral prices would beat T-bills in most windows (about 75% of 10-year starts). Actual: 91.5% of 10-year starts, 100% of 15-year starts.

## Phrasings that overstate the claim
- "Prepaying always wins" or "you will save X": the result is about a fully price-guaranteed contract against T-bills in a mostly low-rate period (data start Dec 1997), not about any particular contract.
- "Funeral homes raise prices 3.3% a year": that is the national index average; a given funeral home and a given set of choices differ.
- "Saving is a losing strategy": T-bills covered more than the funeral in a few start months (maximum coverage 108.4%), and higher-rate periods before 1997 are not in the data.

## The episode must not
- Tell viewers to buy (or not buy) a prepaid funeral plan, or name sellers or funeral homes.
- Forecast funeral prices, inflation or interest rates.

## Data limits
- Index starts Dec 1997: about three independent 10-year periods, almost all in years of low short-term rates. History, not a forecast.
- Taxes on T-bill interest ignored (favors saving), plan fees, trust or insurance structure, cancellation and portability rules (state law) ignored.
- Items funeral homes pass through (cemetery opening and closing, certificates, obituaries) are usually not guaranteed by contracts.
