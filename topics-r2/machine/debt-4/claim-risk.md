# debt-4 claim risk

**Phrasings that would overstate the claim**
- "Waiting never helps." It lowered the payment in 38.2% of past one-year waits, by up to $27.82 a month.
- "Car prices always rise." The median one-year change in the new-vehicle price index was +0.54%, and it fell in some years; the index adjusts for quality, so sticker prices behave differently.
- "Your rate won't drop much." Only the bank survey's typical rate was measured; individual rates depend on credit, lender and dealer promotions.

**What the episode must not say**
- No advice ("buy now", "don't wait"). No forecast of loan rates or car prices. Say "history, not a forecast."

**Data limits**
- Quarterly bank survey of the 60-month new-car rate, 2006-2026 (76 one-year pairs); no credit-union, captive or used-car rates.
- Costs of keeping the old car (repairs, fuel, risk of breakdown) and incentives are not modeled.
- $35,000 and 60 months are fixed; a buyer who waits and saves for a larger down payment is not modeled.
