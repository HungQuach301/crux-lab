# tax: discarded candidates (machine side, topics-r2)

Generated 16 candidate decisions. Kept 4 (tax-1 … tax-4) and **dropped 12**. Candidates on the EXCLUDE list and round-1 machine tax topics were not considered.
Novelty rule used: "answered-with-data" means a source already gives a numeric answer to the same decision. Illustrations with assumed rates are counted as "without data" only when they do not settle the decision.

1. First-time buyers with a $400,000 loan: is the mortgage interest deduction worth anything against the 2026 standard deduction? Dropped as answered-with-data: several 2026 guides compute this exact loan size against the $32,200 standard deduction.
2. Got a late-filing/late-payment penalty: pay it or ask for abatement? Dropped as answered-with-data: abatement rates are published (9-12% of penalties abated, about 8% of eligible taxpayers using first-time abatement).
3. Freelancer: skip the home-office deduction for fear of an audit? Dropped as answered-with-data: audit coverage by return type is widely reported, plus a cited study comparing returns with and without the deduction.
4. Owe the IRS: offer in compromise or installment agreement? Dropped as answered-with-data: acceptance rates from the IRS Data Book are reported (CNBC, Sept 2026).
5. Harvest a loss when only $3,000 a year offsets wages? Dropped as answered-with-data: the inflation-adjusted value of the 1978 limit is computed in several articles.
6. Cash out a 401(k) to pay off card debt? Dropped as answered-with-data (penalty-plus-tax haircut examples, lost-growth estimates). It is also close to the EXCLUDE line on pausing 401(k) contributions to pay cards.
7. Use the $10,000 first-home IRA exception for a down payment? Dropped as answered-with-data: the 1997 limit is already compared with 1997 and current median home prices.
8. Pension lump sum: retire in December or January (rate lookback)? Dropped as answered-with-data: month-to-month lump-sum drops are quantified. It is also more a pension-plan decision than a tax one.
9. Deed the house to the kids now, or leave it to them (step-up in basis)? Dropped as answered-with-data: worked examples of the gain taxed under each route. The real-data angle (home-price index) adds little.
10. Gig drivers: standard mileage rate or actual car expenses? Dropped because the actual-expense side (depreciation, insurance, repairs) has no usable public series, so the result would be mostly assumptions.
11. 24% bracket: municipal bonds or taxable bonds? Dropped because the municipal-yield series available ends in 2016, so there is no data for current years.
12. Buy big-ticket goods before or after tariff changes? Dropped because answering it would require a price forecast, which the channel does not make.
