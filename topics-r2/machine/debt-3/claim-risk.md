# debt-3 claim risk

**Phrasings that would overstate the claim**
- "Your raises won't catch up for seven years." The result is for *average* hourly pay of production and nonsupervisory workers; an individual's raises, promotions or job changes can be much faster or slower.
- "A 35% payment never becomes 28% in four years." True for start months since 1990 in this series only; in the 1970s high inflation did it in as little as 34 months.
- "Stretching is a mistake." The episode measures time, not whether the house is worth it.

**What the episode must not say**
- No advice ("never go above 28%"). No forecast of wages or inflation. Say "history, not a forecast."

**Data limits**
- Monthly, seasonally adjusted average hourly earnings, 1964-2026; gross pay, not take-home pay.
- The payment is principal and interest only; property tax and insurance usually rise, so the true share falls more slowly than shown.
- Start months near the end of the series are dropped when the 25% rise has not happened yet (last start counted: 2021-07).
