- A conventional borrower near 80 percent loan-to-value who renovated, wants to request removal based on a new valuation, and is weighing appraisal costs against a lender fee.

- Treats FHA mortgage insurance and conventional PMI as the same thing, so viewers cannot tell when the charge ends or whether it can be removed.
- Pushes upfront payment or 80/20 structures without a break-even comparison against years of monthly payments, interest, and fees.
